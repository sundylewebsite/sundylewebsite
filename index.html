    About

    Five players, one long groove

    SUN.DYLE is a Fort Wayne, Indiana based 5-piece ensemble that radiates the warmth of jazz, the enchanting vibes of neo-soul, and the rhythmic heartbeats of R&B. Hailing from diverse musical backgrounds, the members of SUN.DYLE converge to create a harmonious blend of genres that persuades the listener to feel, think and transcend.

    The band came together in the summer of 2023 out of jam sessions around downtown Fort Wayne, and put out a four-song debut EP that November. Local radio picked it up, the rooms got bigger, and two records later they’re still chasing the same thing: a groove you can sink into and a horn line you can hum home.

    “I grew up heavy on Amy Winehouse, Ella Fitzgerald … the really epic singers, the ones with voices that make you feel a certain way.” Emma Torres · Whatzup, January 2024
